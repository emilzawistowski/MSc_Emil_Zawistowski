CAVEAT: this check compares the sign of Fz against the sign of TP9
and TP10 INDEPENDENTLY, on the same common-average reference (CAR)
used throughout the main pipeline - it does NOT re-reference to
TP9/TP10 (an earlier version of this script did that and produced
a meaningless result: referencing two channels to their own average
algebraically forces them to sum to zero and be equal-and-opposite
for any input, which is a property of the arithmetic, not a finding
about the EEG). TP9/TP10 also only approximately correspond to the
true mastoids per the CLACS-32 montage documentation, and CAR does
not carry the same textbook guarantee as a nose-tip reference for
this criterion. Interpret as a supportive, secondary diagnostic -
see thesis Limitations.
